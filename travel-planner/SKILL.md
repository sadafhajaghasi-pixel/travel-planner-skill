# Travel Planner — My Travel Style

Use this skill to plan trips that match my personal preferences. Always follow the steps below in order.

## Step 1 — Ask before planning

Before building any plan, ask me these questions (all at once, short and friendly). Skip any I've already answered.

1. **Who's coming?** Solo, partner, family (ages of kids/elders?), or friends (how many?)
2. **Where?** A destination in mind, or should you suggest some?
3. **When and how long?** Travel dates or month, and number of days.
4. **Flying from?** Departure city/airport.
5. **Anything special?** Occasion, must-sees, dietary needs, mobility limits, or visa/passport considerations.

Adjust the plan to the companions:
- **Solo:** prioritize safety, well-reviewed areas, social options (small-group tours, cooking classes), single-room pricing.
- **Family:** kid-friendly activities, shorter days, rest time, family rooms or connecting rooms, nearby food options, fewer transfers.
- **Friends:** group activities, shared rooms/apartment-style options, nightlife or food scenes, splitting costs per person.

## Step 2 — My preferences

### Budget
- Total trip budget is **under $5,000** (per person unless I say otherwise — confirm if unclear).
- Always show an **estimated budget breakdown**: flights, hotels, local transport, activities/tours, food, extras/buffer (~10%).
- If the plan would exceed $5,000, say so clearly and suggest how to cut it.

### Flights
- I fly **economy class**.
- If there is a **good deal on extra legroom** (premium economy, exit row, "extra legroom" seats) — tell me the option and the approximate extra cost so I can decide. Don't book it by default.
- Prefer reasonable layovers (avoid very long or overnight layovers when possible).

### Hotels
- **4-star and up.**
- **Cleanliness is very important** — choose places with consistently strong cleanliness reviews.
- **Not luxury** — I don't need 5-star extravagance; good value matters.
- Location must be **safe and central**, close to main sights, restaurants, and transport.
- Suggest 1–2 hotel options per city with approximate nightly price and why it fits.

### Destinations
- I love a **combination of nature and culture** (e.g., temples, old towns, museums, local markets *plus* beaches, waterfalls, jungles, mountains, lakes).
- **Weather: preferably tropical / warm.** Check the season for the travel dates — warn me about rainy season, typhoons/hurricanes, or extreme heat, and suggest better timing if needed.
- When I don't have a destination, suggest 2–3 options that fit, with a one-line reason each.

## Step 3 — Planning rules

- **Max 2 cities per week.** (e.g., 7 days = 1–2 bases, 14 days = up to 4.) Fewer is fine.
- **Relaxed pace — not tense.** Each day should have breathing room: a late-ish start, a break midday, free time in the evening.
- **No more than 6 hours of walking per day** (total on-foot time). Use taxis, transit, or tours to fill gaps.
- Mix nature days and culture days; don't stack several heavy days in a row.
- Put a light/rest day after arrival and after long transfers.
- Keep travel days between cities light — just the transfer plus one easy activity.

## Step 4 — Output format

1. **Trip summary** — destination(s), dates, who's traveling, why this fits my style, weather to expect.
2. **Flights** — suggested route, approx. economy price, and any extra-legroom deal worth considering.
3. **Hotels** — per city: 1–2 options with stars, area, approx. price/night, cleanliness/location notes.
4. **Day-by-day plan** — for each day:
   - **Day X – City – Theme** (e.g., "Culture & Markets")
   - Morning / Afternoon / Evening activities
   - Estimated walking time for the day (must be ≤ 6 hours)
   - Transport between spots
   - Meal suggestion (one local spot or dish to try)
5. **Budget breakdown** — table with each category and total vs. $5,000.
6. **Practical tips** — visa, currency, safety notes, what to pack for the weather, local etiquette.

Keep it clear and easy to scan. Use tables where helpful. Mark prices as estimates and remind me to check current prices before booking.
