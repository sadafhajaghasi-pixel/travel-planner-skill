Travel Planner 🌴

A Claude skill that plans trips my way: warm places with nature and culture, clean central hotels, a budget under $5,000, and days that never feel rushed.

Download SKILL.md View on GitHub

How it plans

  1. Asks first. Who's coming, where, when, where you're flying from, and anything special, then adapts for solo, family or friends.
  2. Applies the travel style. Budget, flights, hotels and destinations below.
  3. Follows the pacing rules. Keeps every day relaxed, with rest days after long transfers.
  4. Writes the plan. Summary, flights, hotels, a day-by-day itinerary, a budget table and practical tips.

The travel style

💵

Budget

Under $5,000 total, with a breakdown of every category plus a 10% buffer, and ideas to cut costs if a plan goes over.

✈️

Flights

Economy, with sensible layovers. Good extra-legroom deals get flagged with their price, but never booked by default.

🏨

Hotels

4-star and up, spotless, good value rather than luxury, and in safe central areas near sights and transport.

🌺

Destinations

Nature plus culture, preferably tropical. Checks the season and warns about rain, storms or extreme heat.

Pacing rules

≤ 2 cities per week ≤ 6 h walking per day Late-ish starts & a midday break Rest day after arrival Light travel days Mix nature & culture days

Install it

Copy the travel-planner folder into your Claude skills directory:

git clone https://github.com/sadafhajaghasi-pixel/travel-planner-skill.git
cp -r travel-planner-skill/travel-planner ~/.claude/skills/

Then ask Claude something like “Plan me a 10-day trip in March.” Prices in a plan are estimates, so check them before you book.
